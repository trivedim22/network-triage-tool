How to Think Like an Analyst

  1. Observe unusual behaviour — what stands out versus normal for this network?
  2. Identify the source and destination — which device, which system, who owns them?
  3. Determine which protocol is involved — DNS, ICMP, TCP, ARP all mean different things.
  4. Establish whether the activity is expected — backups, updates, and admin tools look noisy too.
  5. Look for supporting indicators — ports, timing, packet sizes, repeated patterns.
  6. Correlate with other telemetry — endpoint logs, firewall, IDS, user reports.
  7. Decide whether further investigation is required — escalate, monitor, or close with notes.

Wireshark → CSV in 5 steps

  1. Open Wireshark and capture traffic in an authorised environment.
  2. Stop the capture when you have enough packets.
  3. Go to File > Export Packet Dissections > As CSV.
  4. Save the packet-list CSV to your computer.
  5. Upload the CSV into this tool (top of this page).
